TrueRate

Know what to charge, whether a contract is worth taking, and what you can afford between contracts.

You're earning $900 a day. How much of that is yours? TrueRate is an app for Australian contractors, freelancers and sole traders, on iPhone and Mac. It turns your hourly or day rate into a real picture of what you keep after income tax, the Medicare levy, GST, super, business expenses and the weeks you don't get paid for.

Contractor decisions

  • Safe to Spend: enter your balance and receipts to estimate what to reserve for tax, GST, super and upcoming expenses, and what remains available.
  • Income calculator: change your rate, working week or time off and watch your take-home move.
  • Contract vs permanent: the salary and super a permanent role would need to match your contract.
  • Should I take it? A contract, how long it runs and the gap after it, against staying where you are. Or compare two offers side by side.
  • Contracts and forecast: working days and expected invoicing by month, with your state's public holidays skipped.
  • Cash runway: how long your money lasts at the drawings you choose, with late payments and gaps between contracts.
  • Payment updates: record receipts and tax, GST and super payments, with editing and undo.
  • What's coming up: every BAS, super deadline and contract ending, with a reminder before each one.
  • Rate finder, time off and what if: work back from the take-home you want, price a break, or try a rate rise next to today.
  • Ways of working: sole trader, company, PAYG contractor, casual or permanent, the same year side by side.
  • For your accountant: your year as a PDF, with every assumption written down.
  • Widgets: Safe to Spend, your contract countdown and the next BAS on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Estimates only. TrueRate provides estimates for planning and information. It is not tax, legal or financial advice, and tax outcomes depend on individual circumstances. Every figure shows the assumptions behind it; confirm your obligations with the Australian Taxation Office or a registered tax agent.

Frequently asked

Which tax year does it use?
2026-27 resident rates by default; 2025-26 is available in Settings. Rates and thresholds are listed under Settings › Rates and thresholds used.
Does it connect to my bank or my accountant's software?
No. You type in your balance and what you've received; nothing is linked to a bank or accounting service, and TrueRate never moves money or lodges anything.
Where is my data kept?
On your devices. With iCloud turned on, your plan is also kept in your own private iCloud database so your iPhone and Mac share it; we never receive it. See the privacy policy.
What does Pro include?
Ongoing Safe to Spend, payment updates and activity history, cash runway, forecast and what-if scenarios, unlimited contracts, the rate finder, time off tools, ways of working, offer extras, benchmarks, reminders, the accountant summary and widgets. It is A$7.99 a month or A$59.99 a year, each starting with a one-week free trial for eligible Apple Accounts, managed and cancelled through the App Store. The income calculator, contract vs permanent, basic offer comparison, the Should I take it? verdict, one contract, the expected monthly forecast and the share card are free. You can also try one personal Safe to Spend preview before subscribing; it gives a dated snapshot that does not update.
I contract through a company or an agency
Both are covered. Choose how you contract in setup or Settings: sole trader, your own company (under the personal services income rules by default, or as a personal services business with a salary, company tax and franked dividends), or paid wages through an agency as a PAYG contractor.
How do I cancel or restore Pro?
Cancel any time in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends. To restore on a new phone, open TrueRate › Settings › TrueRate Pro › Restore purchases.

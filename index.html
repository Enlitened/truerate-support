TrueRate

Know what your contract income is actually worth.

You're earning $900 a day. How much of that is yours? TrueRate is an iPhone app for Australian contractors, freelancers and sole traders. It turns your hourly or day rate into a real picture of what you keep after income tax, the Medicare levy, GST, super, business expenses and the weeks you don't get paid for.

What it does

  • Safe to spend — enter your business balance and see what's left after money reserved for tax, GST, super and upcoming expenses.
  • Income calculator — change your rate, working week or time off and watch your take-home move.
  • Contract vs permanent — the salary and super a permanent role would need to match your contract.
  • Should I take it? — a contract, how long it runs and the gap after it, against staying where you are. Or compare two offers side by side.
  • Contracts and forecast — working days and expected invoicing by month, with your state's public holidays skipped.
  • What's coming up — every BAS, super deadline and contract ending, with a reminder before each one.
  • Rate finder, time off and what if — work back from the take-home you want, price a break, or try a rate rise next to today.
  • Ways of working — sole trader, company, PAYG contractor, casual or permanent, the same year side by side.
  • For your accountant — your year as a PDF, with every assumption written down.
  • Widgets — Safe to Spend, your contract countdown and the next BAS on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Estimates only. TrueRate provides estimates for planning and information. It is not tax, legal or financial advice, and tax outcomes depend on individual circumstances. Every figure shows the assumptions behind it; confirm your obligations with the Australian Taxation Office or a registered tax agent.

Frequently asked

Which tax year does it use?
2026–27 resident rates by default; 2025–26 is available in Settings. Rates and thresholds are listed under Settings › Rates and assumptions.
Does it connect to my bank or my accountant's software?
No. You type in your balance and what you've received; nothing is linked and nothing is uploaded.
Where is my data kept?
On your iPhone only. See the privacy policy.
What does Pro include?
Safe to Spend, What's coming up and reminders, the accountant summary, unlimited contracts, forecasts and what-if scenarios, the rate finder, the time off planner, ways of working and widgets. It is A$7.99 a month or A$59.99 a year, each starting with a one-week free trial for eligible Apple Accounts, managed and cancelled through the App Store. The income calculator, contract vs permanent, offer comparison, one contract and the share card are free.
I contract through a company or an agency
Both are covered. Choose how you contract in setup or Settings: sole trader, your own company (under the personal services income rules by default, or as a personal services business with a salary, company tax and franked dividends), or paid wages through an agency as a PAYG contractor.
How do I cancel or restore Pro?
Cancel any time in the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends. To restore on a new phone, open TrueRate › Settings › TrueRate Pro › Restore purchases.

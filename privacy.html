Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

TrueRate does not collect any data about you.

What TrueRate stores

Everything you enter in TrueRate — your rate, how you work, your time off, your GST and super arrangements, your expenses, your tax settings, your contracts and the balance and payments you type into Safe to Spend — is stored on your device, in the app's own local database. Your chosen tax year, unfinished setup and offer-comparison drafts, lightweight payment history and optional personal-preview snapshot are stored the same way.

In the forthcoming Mac/iCloud release, if you are signed in to iCloud, that database is also kept in your own private iCloud database, so TrueRate on your iPhone and your Mac share one plan. Apple stores it under your Apple Account and its encryption; we do not receive or access it, and it is never on a server of ours. To keep your data on one device only, turn off iCloud for TrueRate in your device's iCloud settings.

A few settings sit alongside it in the app's preferences on the device: whether you finished setting up, whether reminders are on, the role and level you last looked at in rate benchmarks, whether this device has TrueRate Pro, and a count of the days you have used the app and the times it gave you a result, used only to decide when to show Apple's standard rating prompt and never sent anywhere.

TrueRate never asks for your tax file number, bank login, account numbers or any identity document, and has nowhere to put them.

If you add a TrueRate widget, the app keeps a small copy of the figures that widget shows (such as Safe to Spend, your current contract's name, end date and what's left to bill on it, and the next BAS) in a shared container on your device, so the widget can read it. Those figures then appear wherever you place the widget; the amounts are marked private, so iOS can hide them while your iPhone is locked.

If you turn on reminders, TrueRate schedules them on your device through iOS. A reminder names the deadline, such as a BAS or a contract ending (by the name you gave the contract), and never includes a dollar figure. Like any notification, it can appear on your Lock Screen. Reminders stop if Pro ends.

What TrueRate sends

Nothing about you. TrueRate has no server, no account and no network code of its own. It cannot send your figures anywhere except your own private iCloud database, described above, which only your devices signed in to your Apple Account can read.

Apart from the private iCloud sync described above, your figures leave the app when you choose to share them. The shareable rate card and the summary for your accountant are made on your iPhone or Mac and handed to the system's share sheet; where they go from there (a message, an email, a file) is up to you, and TrueRate never sees it. The rate card uses clearly labelled example figures by default. If you choose personal figures it carries your rate and what it works out to, never your balance or contracts; if you choose to add the going rate for your role, it also shows the role, level and state you picked and the range for them. The accountant summary is written to a temporary file for the share sheet to pick up, which iOS clears out over time.

The one exception is the App Store itself: if you subscribe to TrueRate Pro, Apple's own StoreKit talks to Apple to complete and verify the purchase. That conversation is between you and Apple; it carries nothing about your finances, and its result stays on the device.

The Support, Privacy policy and Terms of use links, and Manage subscription, open in Safari or the App Store, outside TrueRate. Nothing from the app goes with them.

Public website calculator

The calculator loads bundled calculation data from this website. Your inputs stay in the browser and are not sent with that request or saved by the calculator. The site adds no analytics, tracking SDK or cookies. The hosting provider receives ordinary page and file requests, including normal network metadata, under its own policy.

Purchases

TrueRate offers one optional subscription, TrueRate Pro, handled entirely by the App Store. TrueRate never sees your name, your Apple Account, your payment details or your purchase history. It asks the App Store one question — whether this device is entitled to Pro — and the App Store answers on the device.

What TrueRate does not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is ever requested.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, and no data shared with any third party for any purpose.
  • No bank feeds, no accounting-software connections, no location, contacts or photos.

Backups

If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, your TrueRate data is included in that backup, under Apple's terms and encryption rather than ours. TrueRate does not manage or read those backups.

Deleting your data

Settings › Start over erases your profile, balance, contracts, activity history, setup draft and personal-preview snapshot, from this device and from every device that shares your plan through iCloud; it also takes your figures off any widgets, cancels any reminders and forgets your rate benchmark choice. A minimal dated record that the one personal preview was used remains, with no amounts, so Start over cannot reset the allowance. Removing all TrueRate data from iCloud can remove that record too. Deleting the app removes what TrueRate has stored on that device; the copy in your iCloud stays until you start over or remove TrueRate's data in your iCloud settings.

Not advice

TrueRate provides estimates for planning and information only. It is not tax, legal or financial advice. Confirm your obligations with the Australian Taxation Office or a registered tax agent.

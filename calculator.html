The comparison counts cash plus super. Paid leave is already included in a salary; contractor leave reduces billings. It is not added again as a separate benefit.

What this estimate assumes

2026-27 Australian resident, single-person tax settings. Sole trader / ABN, GST registered with GST on top, self-funded super at 12% of ex-GST billings (subject to the contribution cap). A 52-week year, five sick days and ten public holidays, plus your selected leave and gaps. Business expenses are ex-GST. Permanent salary includes paid leave and has 12% employer super on top. No bonus, salary sacrifice, other income, other deductions, dependants or capped study-loan balance are modelled.

Study-loan repayments, Medicare levy and surcharge use the same rules as the app. The salary-side standard work deduction is applied where eligible. The calculator offers a limited set of exact inputs; it does not interpolate tax estimates between them.

Estimates for planning only, not tax, legal or financial advice. Actual work patterns, tax circumstances, timing and cash-flow risks can change the decision. Confirm your obligations with the ATO or a registered tax professional.
